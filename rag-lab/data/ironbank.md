# The Ironbank of Erebor

## Overview
The **Ironbank** is the most secure and formidable repository of wealth, artifacts, and precious metals in Middle-earth. Located deep within the subterranean reaches of the Lonely Mountain (Erebor), it serves as a neutral ground for various races to store their most treasured assets under the watchful eyes of the Dwarven Lords. While not officially part of the Kingdom Under the Mountain's treasury, its operations are inextricably linked to the prosperity of the Dwarvenking.

## Geography and Location
The Ironbank is situated in the deepest, most inaccessible chambers of Erebor, far below the Great Hall.

### The Vault Chambers
The core of the bank consists of several concentric rings of vaults:
- **The Outer Ring (The Bronze Tier):** Stores common metals, trade goods, and bulk ores. Accessible to authorized merchants and representatives of the Blue Mountains.
- **The Silver Tier:** Holds precious gemstones, fine jewelry, and silverwork. Requires high-level clearance from the Dwarvenking's council.
- **The Mithril Vault (The Inner Sanctum):** The most secure chamber, containing only the rarest materials like Mithril, Silmaril shards (if any legends hold true), and ancient relics of the First Age. Access is restricted to the King himself and his most trusted guards.

### The Gatehouse
A massive, magically reinforced gate forged from dark iron and enchanted with runes of warding. It is guarded by the *Iron Sentinels*, an elite unit of Dwarven warriors trained specifically in anti-siege and anti-magic warfare.

### The Deep Arteries
A network of narrow, winding tunnels that connect the vaults to the main halls of Erebor. These tunnels are equipped with various traps, including pit falls, heavy stone crushers, and pressurized steam vents.

## Demographics
While not a city itself, the Ironbank is populated by a permanent staff:

- **The Iron Bankers (Dwarves):** Primarily from the Line of Durin. They are masters of accounting, metallurgy, and security.
- **The Archivists (Noldor/Sindar Elves):** A small group of Elven scholars who maintain the records of deposits, often using ancient scripts and languages to ensure longevity of information.

- **The Sentinels (Dwarven Warriors):** The primary security force.
- **Contracted Mercenaries (Men/Easterlings/Haradrim):** Occasionally used for external escort duties, though they are never allowed within the Silver Tier or deeper.

## History

### The Age of Shadows
During the era of Smaug the Terrible, the Ironbank did not exist in its current form. There were only scattered hoards of gold and gems. However, even then, the concept of a "centralized" repository was whispered about among the Dwarven refugees.

### The Reclaiming of Erebor
Following the death of Smaug and the return of Thorin Oakenshield, the reconstruction of the mountain began. The Ironbank was conceived as a way to stabilize the economy of the rebuilt kingdom and provide a safe haven for the wealth flowing from the Long Lake and beyond.

### The War of the Ring
During the Great War, the Ironbank underwent a massive expansion. Many treasures from Gondor and Rohan were moved to the safety of Erebor, fearing the rise of Sauron. This period saw the establishment of the "Neutrality Pact," ensuring that the Bank would remain uninvolved in the conflicts of the surface world, provided its walls remained unbreached.

## Economy and Trade
The Ironbank is the engine of Dwarven economic dominance.

### Currency and Deposits
- **Gold/Silver/Platinum Weights:** Most deposits are recorded by weight rather than specific coin counts.
- **Artifact Escrow:** The bank holds significant historical artifacts in trust for various lineages.
- **Interest Rates:** Calculated in "Stone-units," a unique Dwarven metric based on the value of precious ore production over time.

### Major Trading Partners
- **The Lonely Lake (Esgaroth):** Primary source of grain and fish trade goods.
- **Dale:** The main hub for commerce and the primary interface between the Bank and the outside world.
- **Mirkwood (Elvenking's Realm):** Occasional exchange of woodland resources and medicinal herbs for precious metals.

## Security Measures

### Runes of Warding
Every vault door is inscribed with complex runes that react to unauthorized presence. These include:
- **Detection Runes:** Sense the heat or magical signature of intruders.
- **Locking Run					Runes:** Prevent physical manipulation of the mechanisms.
- **Alarm Runes:** Trigger loud, resonant bells throughout the mountain if breached.

### The Sentinel Guard
The Iron Sentinels use specialized equipment:
- **Heavy Plate Armor:** Forged in the deepest smithies.
- **Runed Shields:** Capable of absorbing magical impacts.
- **Short Spears and War Hammers:** Optimized for close-quarters combat in narrow tunnels.

## Fun Facts
- It is rumored that a single Mithril coin could buy an entire fleet of ships in Dale.
- The "Great Ledger" is said to be written on sheets of beaten silver, making it indestructible by fire or water.
- Every decade, the "Audit of Ages" occurs, where every single item in the Outer Ring is recounted.
- Legend says that if the Ironbank ever fails, the very foundations of Erebor will crumble.
